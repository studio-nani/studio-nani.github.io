STUDIO NANI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Studio Nani handles information for its websites, apps, and internal creator tools, including tools that connect to third-party platforms such as TikTok or YouTube.

2. Information we process

Depending on the tool, Studio Nani may process:

  • Account authorization data provided by a third-party platform login flow, such as access tokens or account identifiers.
  • Media files, captions, titles, descriptions, and publishing metadata selected by an authorized Studio Nani operator.
  • Basic technical logs needed to verify upload status, diagnose errors, and maintain the tool.

3. How we use information

We use this information only to operate Studio Nani products and creator workflows, including uploading authorized content, checking upload status, troubleshooting failures, and maintaining internal records.

4. Token storage

OAuth tokens for internal creator tools are stored only in Studio Nani-controlled local or server environments. They are not sold, rented, or shared with unrelated third parties.

5. Third-party services

When a Studio Nani tool connects to a platform such as TikTok or YouTube, that platform may process data under its own privacy policy and developer terms. Studio Nani uses official APIs or authorized login flows where available.

6. Data sharing

Studio Nani does not sell personal information. We may share information only when required to operate a requested third-party integration, comply with law, protect our rights, or maintain security.

7. Deletion and contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests related to Studio Nani tools, contact: studionani.official@gmail.com
